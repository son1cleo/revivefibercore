Circular textile manufacturing keeps valuable materials in use for longer and cuts disposal costs. For fiber-based factories, this means recovering offcuts, sorting by composition, and reintegrating processed fiber into new production.

## Why It Matters

- Lower landfill dependency
- Better raw material stability
- Improved sustainability reporting

By combining data tracking with process control, manufacturers can reduce waste while improving output consistency.
