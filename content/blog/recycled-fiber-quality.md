High-performance recycled fiber is possible when sourcing and processing standards are strict. The quality conversation should move beyond assumptions and focus on measurable thresholds.

## Key Metrics

- Fiber length distribution
- Blend consistency
- Tensile durability
- Moisture balance

With proper preprocessing and testing, recycled blends can meet premium textile requirements.
